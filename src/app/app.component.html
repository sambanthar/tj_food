TJ HOMEMADE PRODUCTS was born right in our home kitchen out of a deep passion to protect our families from harmful chemicals, synthetic food colors, artificial taste boosters, and harsh chemical cosmetics.

Every single masala is roasted slowly in traditional pans to bring out deep, authentic flavours. Our herbal bath powders and hair cleansers are formulated according to time-tested ancestral recipes that our grandmothers trusted for generations.

When you order from us, you are directly helping a small home enterprise grow. We promise uncompromised purity, unmatched hygiene, and honest homemade goodness in every single pack.
